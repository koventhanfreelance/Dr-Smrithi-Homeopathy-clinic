What happens at your first homeopathy consultation

  1. Book a time

    Call, WhatsApp or use the form. Choose an in-clinic visit or an online consultation.

  2. A detailed first talk

    The doctor asks about your main concern, past health, sleep, food, stress and mood. Bring old reports and a list of your medicines.

  3. A plan made for you

    Your remedy is chosen for you as a person. You will know how to take it and when to come back.

  4. Follow-ups

    Regular check-ins, in person or online, to see how you are doing and adjust the plan.

What homeopathy can and cannot do for you

How we work

  • Remedies are chosen for you, after a full consultation.
  • You keep taking any medicine your doctor has prescribed.
  • Changes are often gradual, and follow-ups track them.
  • Every person responds differently. No result is guaranteed.

When to go to a hospital

  • Severe breathlessness, chest pain or fainting.
  • High fever that will not come down, or a fit.
  • Heavy bleeding, or blood in vomit or stool.
  • Any sudden, severe symptom. Call 108.

How to choose the best homeopathy doctor in Chennai

Whoever you choose, these are the things worth checking before you book.

  • A recognised degree and registration. Look for a BHMS degree and registration with the state homoeopathy council.
  • A detailed first consultation. A good homeopath asks about your whole health, not only one symptom.
  • A clear plan. You should know how to take your medicine and when to come back.
  • Honesty about limits. No one should promise a guaranteed cure, or ask you to stop prescribed medicines.
  • Easy to reach. Clear timings, a real address, and online follow-ups if you need them.
  • Recent patient reviews. Read a few on Google to see how patients describe their care.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Dr Smrithi Homeopathy Clinic?

The clinic is at No. 107, 2nd Trust Main Road, Mandavelipakkam, Chennai 600028. It is a short drive from Mylapore, Raja Annamalai Puram, Adyar and Alwarpet.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes. In-clinic visits are by appointment only, so every patient gets unhurried time with the doctor. You can book by phone, WhatsApp or the form on the contact page.

What are the clinic timings?

The clinic is open Monday to Saturday, 10 AM to 2 PM and 4 PM to 8 PM, and on Sunday from 10 AM to 2 PM.

Can I consult the doctor online?

Yes. Online consultations are available on a call, so you can speak to the doctor from home or from outside Chennai. Your medicines can then be delivered to your doorstep.

How long is the first consultation?

The first consultation is longer than a follow-up, because the doctor asks about your health history, routine, sleep, food and stress before choosing a remedy.

Should I stop my current medicines?

No. Please do not stop any medicine your doctor has prescribed without talking to them. Tell Dr. Smrithi about everything you take so she can plan your care safely.

Is homeopathy suitable for children?

Yes. Dr. Smrithi sees children as well as adults, both in the clinic and online. A parent or guardian should come along or join the online call, and should bring any reports and a list of medicines the child takes.

How do I choose the best homeopathy doctor in Chennai?

Check that the doctor holds a BHMS degree and is registered, that the first consultation is detailed and unhurried, and that the clinic explains your plan and follow-ups clearly. Reading recent patient reviews on Google also helps.
