Dr. Smrithi Chellama, homeopathy doctor in Chennai

Training

During her BHMS, Dr. Smrithi took part in research, case studies and clinical internships to learn homeopathic principles and how to apply them with real patients.

After graduating, she worked alongside experienced homeopaths to build her clinical skills, which sharpened how she understands each patient's problem and chooses a remedy. She keeps learning through ongoing professional study.

Her approach to patient care

"I strongly believe in taking the time to listen to my patients' concerns, understanding their medical history, and doing a detailed assessment to find the underlying causes of their problems."

Homeopathy looks at the person as a whole, not only the symptom. So Dr. Smrithi considers your physical, mental and emotional health together and builds a plan for you. The aim is long-term wellbeing, not a quick fix.

Clear, two-way conversation

You will know what your plan is, what to expect and when to come back. Dr. Smrithi encourages patients to take an active part in their care, because working together gives the best chance of progress.

Working alongside your other doctors

If you already see another doctor or take regular medicines, keep doing so and tell Dr. Smrithi about them. If a problem needs tests or a specialist, she will tell you.

Questions about Dr. Smrithi

How long has Dr. Smrithi been practising?

What happens at the first consultation?

The first consultation is a detailed conversation. The doctor asks about your main concern, past illnesses, medicines you take, sleep, food, stress and mood, and then explains your plan.

Can I see Dr. Smrithi online?

Yes. Online consultations are available on a call, and medicines can be delivered to your home after the consultation.
